⚠️ HIGH RISK WARNING: SHORT SELLING IS NOT A LONG-TERM STRATEGY
Short selling carries theoretically infinite downside risk because stock prices can rise indefinitely. Short positions are subject to borrow fees, margin interest, and extreme asymmetric tail-risk (short squeezes).
"Markets can remain irrational longer than you can remain solvent." — John Maynard Keynes
"Short selling is a tactical sprint, not a marathon. Get in, hit the target or cut loss, and get out."
