📌 Key Financial & Tax Takeaways:
  • Double Taxation Relief (DTA): Under Article 22 of the UK-Pakistan Double Taxation Agreement, Pakistan WHT (10%) can be claimed directly against your UK HMRC liability to avoid paying tax twice.
  • Personal Savings Allowance (PSA): Higher rate taxpayers (£50,271–£125,140) receive a £500 tax-free savings allowance. Basic rate taxpayers receive £1,000.
